YouTube Cookies

Required for server/VPS deployments.
YouTube blocks downloads from datacenter IPs. You need to provide cookies from a logged-in browser session.

How to export cookies:
1. Install a browser extension like "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY" (Chrome/Firefox).
2. Log into YouTube in your browser.
3. Navigate to youtube.com and export the cookies as a .txt file (Netscape format).
